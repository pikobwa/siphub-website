Our Mission

Bridging the gap between potential and opportunity.

At SIP Hub, we walk alongside neurodivergent children and youth as they transition into mainstream education, higher learning, and the digital economy. Through hands-on ICT training, tailored mentorship, and strong partnerships with schools and employers, we help them move from struggle to success.

  • Personalized academic support that fits diverse learning styles
  • Practical digital and workplace readiness pathways
  • Teacher training and inclusive classroom strategies
Our Story

Built from lived experience, community trust, and practical action.

At SIP Hub, we believe every learner deserves a clear path to independence, education, and economic inclusion. Founded on the ground in Kibera and expanding across Nairobi and East Africa, our team combines specialized expertise in special needs education, community development, and information technology to create practical, life-changing pathways for neurodivergent youth.

What We Do

Programs designed for growth at every stage.

Early Intervention & Pre-Academic Readiness

Training young neurodivergent children in essential pre-academic skills, behavioral adaptations, and classroom socialization to support successful integration into mainstream classrooms.

Personalized Academic Support

Ages 4–16. One-on-one tutoring and homework support tailored to each learner’s strengths, needs, and pace.

Pre-Vocational Training (Youth)

Tailored ICT foundational training, digital literacy, and adaptive soft skills for neurodivergent youth in partnership with the Edmund Rice Centre Nairobi.

Training of Trainers

Capacity-building workshops and inclusive curriculum delivery frameworks for schools, foundations, and educators working to champion inclusive education.

Digital & Pre-Vocational Pathways

Ages 15–35. ICT literacy, creative tech tools, and workplace readiness training for a strong transition into college and the digital economy.

Inclusive Teacher Training

We partner with educators to equip them with practical special education strategies, adaptive teaching tools, and supportive classroom management techniques.

Higher Education & Economic Inclusion

SIP Hub has established a strong, verified track record of professional collaboration and transformative learner outcomes across East Africa.

  • 2 alumni have successfully transitioned to Strathmore University and launched a sustainable entrepreneurship venture manufacturing and selling customized carpets.
  • 1 current student was recently trained and successfully joined Strathmore University under the Neurodivergents in Tech cohort.
  • Key institutional collaborations include Strathmore University, Beryl Creek School, Victorious Centre of Excellence, and Eagle Wings Foundation in Tanzania.
